What is the Venus?

Venus is the second planet from the Sun

Fun Facts about Venus

  • Venus has no liquid water, and its atmosphere is far thicker and denser than that of any other rocky body in the solar system

  • The atmoshere is composed mostly of carbon dioxide and has a thick cloud layer of sulfuric acid that spans the whole planet.

  • Venus is one of the four terrestrial planets in the Solar System. It is similar to Earth in size and mass and is often described as Earth's twin.
