Fun Facts about the Moon

  • The Moon is slowly moving away from Earth, getting about an inch farther away from each other.

  • The moon likely formed after a Mars-sized collided with Earth several billions years ago

  • The Moon has a core, mantle, and crust. Long ago the Moon had active volcanos, but today they are all dormant and have not erupted for millions of years.

WHy is the Moon is important?

The Moon is vitual to Earth because its gravity controls our ocean tides, keeps our climate stable, and lengthens our days into a predictable 24-hour cycle.
