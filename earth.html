Fun Facts about the Earth

  • Earth is the third planet from the sun and the only astronomical object known to harbor life.

  • Almost all of Earth's water is contained in its ocean, which covers 70.8% of Earth's crust. The remaining 29.2% of Earth's crust is land.

  • Earth has a dynamic atmosphere, which sustains Earth's surface conditions and protects it from most meteoroids and ultraviolet lightr at entry.
